5. **Invented words by condensation** — blending evocative roots from both languages (2-3 syllables max)

### French linguistic sensitivity checks applied

For each candidate, I verified:
- No embarrassing homophones in French (e.g., "cul", "con", "bite", "pute", "foutre", "chier", "pet", etc.)
- No slang or argot meanings that would be mocked

- The name does not evoke an existing French brand/product that would confuse

## New Recommendation: Imbura

**Score**: 44/45 — highest in both the original benchmark and this complement

**Why Imbura over the alternatives**:

1. **Perfect bilingual resonance**: In English, "imbue" means to permeate with a quality or feeling. In French, "imbu" (adjective) means "imbued/infused with" (e.g., "imbu de culture" = steeped in culture). Both languages independently understand the root.

2. **Zero French sensitivity issue**: Unlike Culma ("cul"), Imbura has zero embarrassing substring in French. "Imbu" is a legitimate, positive French word.

3. **Exceptional availability**: ALL four major TLDs free (.app, .com, .io, .co). Zero App Store. Zero Play Store. Zero trademark conflict found. This is extraordinarily rare for a 6-letter name.

4. **Phonetic warmth**: The nasal "im-", soft "bu", and open "-ra" create a warm, rounded sound perfectly matching the beige/calm brand identity.

5. **Concision**: 6 letters, 3 syllables — shorter than Steepen (7), easier to type than Macena, more distinctive than Fondma.

6. **Semantic depth**: Beyond imbue/imbiber, "imbura" means "rain" in Kinyarwanda — poetic association with knowledge falling gently and nourishing growth.

7. **Over Macena**: While Macena has beautiful Maecenas resonance, it shares a Brazilian surname which creates SEO noise. Imbura has zero noise.

8. **Over Steepra**: Steepra requires knowing English "steep". Imbura works in both languages independently.

9. **Over Fondma**: Fondma's "nsm" cluster is slightly awkward to pronounce. Imbura flows more naturally.

**Recommended domain**: `imbura.app` (or `imbura.com` since it is also free — exceptional)

**Visual identity suggestion**: Warm beige background, soft indigo accent. Logo: lowercase "imbura" in a rounded sans-serif (Inter, Nunito). Icon: stylized raindrop being absorbed into a surface — representing knowledge gently permeating.

**Next steps if approved**:
1. Register `imbura.com` + `imbura.app` immediately (both free — rare window)
2. Formal trademark search TESS (USPTO) + TMView (EUIPO) classes 9 and 42
3. Register @imbura on X, Instagram, TikTok
